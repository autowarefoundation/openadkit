# Before v2.0.0

v2.0.0 is the first Open AD Kit release with a version, and runs Autoware 2.0.0.
It upgrades none of what the name covered before it:

- The 2022 [documentation](https://autowarefoundation.github.io/open-ad-kit-docs/)'s
  "version 1.0" and "version 2.0", a driverless-bus reference design.
- The Docker images of the Autoware repository from 2024.
- The unversioned `main` builds of this repository on Autoware 1.x. v2.0.0 does
  not read their settings or keep their image and service names, and nothing
  updates their images on GHCR.
